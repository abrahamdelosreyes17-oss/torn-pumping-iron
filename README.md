# Torn Pumping Iron

A Tampermonkey script for [Torn](https://www.torn.com) that plans your gym training and scouts your fights.

- **Plan:** it recommends how to train for your stats and money (steady Xanax + refill, a goal, or a happy jump) and warns you when an option isn't worth it.
- **Today:** what to take and when, which stat and how many trains, in Torn time. It re-times itself when you're late.
- **Buy:** the cheapest Xanax, points, candy and EDVDs across the item market, bazaars and the points market, with how many to take from each seller and a link straight to the listing.
- **Gym page:** the stat to train is outlined, with the number of trains and the expected gain. "Fill" types the number into Torn's box; you press TRAIN.
- **Torn Eye:** on profiles, faction lists, wars and the attack page, a chip says Stomp, Good, Tough or Can't win, with your chance to win and the HP you'd keep. It also has a war view sorted by who's attackable now and who's out of hospital next.
- **Discord (optional):** a small free service that tags you in Discord when a step is due.

> Status: in development. Install instructions, key usage tables and the rules it keeps are written at the first release.

Stat estimates for players you haven't fought can come from [FFScouter](https://ffscouter.com) if you connect it. Bazaar prices come from [TornW3B](https://weav3r.dev).
